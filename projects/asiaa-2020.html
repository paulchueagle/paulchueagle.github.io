Research Summary

This research explores the thermodynamic properties of the Intracluster Medium (ICM) in galaxy clusters to investigate the heating mechanisms in Weak Cool Cores (WCC). Cool core clusters suffer from high radiative cooling rates, yet observations show they do not cool as fast as predicted. This points to active heating mechanisms counteracting the cooling.

Our project focused on identifying whether the heating in WCC clusters is driven by **Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN) feedback** or **gas sloshing**. While AGN feedback heats clusters through jets and bubbles, it is difficult to detect in WCC. Gas sloshing, a result of cluster mergers, is an alternative mechanism. To distinguish between them, we analyzed the nature of gas density perturbations: if driven by sloshing, the process is expected to be **isobaric** (constant pressure); if driven by sound waves/shocks, it is expected to be **adiabatic**.

Weak Cool Core (WCC) Heating

Analyzing heating sources in galaxy clusters that have weak cool cores, comparing the spatial distribution of thermodynamic features like temperature, entropy, and metallicity.

Perturbation Analysis

Fitting profiles and calculating ratios between positive and negative excess gas density regions to characterize the physical process (Isobaric vs Adiabatic).

Step 1: Chandra Data Download

We downloaded X-ray archival observations from the Chandra X-ray database, selecting clusters with high exposure time and appropriate redshift. Primary target clusters included WCC systems such as A2142, A3571, A2657, A1651, A3266, and A0576.

Thermodynamic Slope & Ratios

By examining the ratio of thermodynamic parameters between the positive and negative density excess regions (slopes of regression lines), we can distinguish the nature of the perturbations:

Weak Cool Core (WCC)

Average fitted slope across WCC targets:

Pressure Ratio 0.92 ± 0.03
1.0 (Isobaric)
Temperature Ratio 1.20 ± 0.05
Entropy Ratio 1.43 ± 0.10
Abundance Ratio 0.78 ± 0.08

Comparison parameters (Ueda et al. 2020):

Pressure Ratio 0.98 ± 0.05
1.0 (Isobaric)
Temperature Ratio 1.18 ± 0.05
Entropy Ratio 1.38 ± 0.09
Abundance Ratio 1.24 ± 0.28
  • Theoretical Models: If the gas sloshing is slow and balanced, it is isobaric, meaning the pressure ratio of excess regions should be very close to 1.0 (marked in red).
  • WCC Characteristics: Our analysis shows that WCC clusters yield a combined pressure slope of 0.92 ± 0.03, indicating that while gas sloshing is active, the thermodynamic state is not perfectly isobaric.
  • Physical Interpretation: Weak cool core perturbations appear to be transitional, potentially involving a combination of isothermal sloshing and adiabatic processes (such as sound waves or minor shocks). Perfect isothermal sloshing is ruled out since the temperature ratio is significantly above 1.0.

Applied Technical Competency

  • Scientific Computing (Python) Developed Python analysis scripts utilizing NumPy, SciPy, and AstroPy to read, extract, and statistically fit thermodynamic profiles from X-ray datasets.
  • Astronomical Pipelines & CLI Workflows Configured complex environment pipelines utilizing the CIAO software package and Sherpa fitting library, automating point-source removal and elliptical modeling.
  • Spectral Fitting & Modeling Fitted multi-temperature plasma models (such as XSPEC APEC models) to extract temperature, electron density, abundance, and calculate derived thermodynamics.
